# 1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT

The most secure implementable design is a synthesis, **MORTSAFE** (a mortsafe is the iron cage locked over a Victorian grave to stop body-snatchers). Skeleton: CENOTAPH's 2-of-3 share quorum — with its fatal third leg, the public timelock share, deleted and replaced by CAIRN's recipient-held share. Load-bearing move: LICHGATE's red team's fix, promoted to a wall — every per-recipient bundle is first encrypted to a key only that recipient holds, so custodians gate the *timing* of delivery and can never read content. Condition layer: THREE ENVELOPES' "the signature is the condition, the chain is only a venue," hardened by its red team's bimodal-liveness fix. Custody hygiene: CAIRN's red-team rule — institutions hold key shares only, never ciphertext. Runners-up lost on their own red teams: LICHGATE — an AND of two young networks dies when either dies, and re-arming accretes decryption capability forever on undeletable storage; CAIRN — its flagship revocation claim was false (share and ciphertext co-located) and its collusion price was two clerks, not two institutions; CENOTAPH — the public timelock share turns 2-of-3 into 1-of-2 at a known date and fakes termination; THREE ENVELOPES — all three custodians are subpoena-reachable humans one proceeding can reach, and a stolen vault suppresses it silently forever.

# 2. THE ARCHITECTURE

**Cryptographic layering, per armed disclosure:**

1. **Recipient lockbox (inner).** At arming, each recipient receives a kit containing a fresh keypair (hybrid X25519 + ML-KEM-768 — classical plus post-quantum key agreement, matching the project's hybrid-signature discipline). The prepared `.sealproof` bundle is encrypted to that public key. No coalition of custodians, node operators, courts, or thieves can ever read it; the worst possible outcome is early *delivery* to the person the user already consented to tell. Public-release bundles have no recipient, hence no inner layer — a separate, explicitly higher-risk class (staged last).
2. **Timing lock (outer).** The lockboxed bundle is AEAD-wrapped (authenticated encryption — tampering is detected) under a fresh random key R, never derived from W. R is Shamir-split 2-of-3 (any two pieces rebuild it; one piece alone is mathematically zero information):
   - **S_E — executor:** a named institution with succession (law firm or escrow agent), prefunded multi-decade engagement. Its envelope holds the share ONLY — no ciphertext, so a photographing clerk gets nothing and stale envelopes are worthless after rotation. Shares are hand-copied or written to smartcard, never rendered through a printer pipeline (spool files and printer flash leak).
   - **S_N — network:** encrypted to a threshold-decryption network (TACo-class: n independent operators, any k of whom can jointly decrypt; the release condition they enforce is code they agree to run — policy, not math, and priced as such). Policy: release only after verified heartbeat silence and no terminate.
   - **S_R — recipient:** inside the kit, beside the lockbox private key, an M-DISC (archival optical disc) of the outer ciphertext, the offline verifier page, and a plain-language claim card.
3. **Ciphertext placement.** Per-recipient ciphertext may mirror to Autonomi — safe even under total custody collapse, because the lockbox leaves it readable by the recipient alone, forever. Public-release ciphertext stays on executor- and user-held media by default, NOT on permanent public storage, so terminate can genuinely claw it back; the consent gate states this trade and lets the user choose permanence instead.

**Condition.** A heartbeat is a statement {switch-id, epoch, monotonic sequence number} signed with a vault-derived hybrid Ed25519+ML-DSA key (fresh HKDF label — a derivation name that cannot collide with any existing key). It is posted monthly as generic calldata (raw transaction data, no contract logic) from a clean wallet, and OpenTimestamped (a free Bitcoin-anchored timestamp proof). The signature is the condition; any channel can carry it; chain death changes nothing; keeping verification off-chain sidesteps CENOTAPH's fatal gas problem (no Ed25519 precompile on Arbitrum, ML-DSA unverifiable on-chain). **Bimodal liveness:** annually the executor also takes direct human proof of life; once silence begins, the only accepted resets are a fresh signature or a pre-issued recovery card (pre-signed "hold N months" statements stored outside the vault) — never an unverified phone call.

The quorum topology is the condition's error-corrector, both directions. Vault thief forging beats forever (THREE ENVELOPES' critical break): executor + recipient fire anyway on failed proof of life. Spurious silence — coma, lost vault: the executor's contact ladder plus recovery card catches it, and the executor can always delay by withholding S_E. No single signal fires or suppresses alone.

**Trust map.** User alone: arm, beat, terminate, rotate; W never leaves the vault. Any one shareholder alone (including k colluding network nodes): one share, nothing. Any two of {executor, network, recipient}: control timing. Any coalition excluding the recipient: can never read a per-recipient bundle. Recipient plus either leg: that recipient's own bundle, early — the accepted floor. Executor plus network: early release of public-release bundles only. Chain and Autonomi: bulletin board and blob store; learn pseudonymous cadence and padded sizes; can censor, never forge or decrypt. Worst case anywhere: the armed, itemized subset — never W, never unarmed units, never seal validity.

**Flows.**
- **Arm:** consent gate itemizes every unit per recipient and prints two hard sentences verbatim: "termination is best-effort against two defecting shareholders" and, for public bundles, "early release of this bundle is readable by the world." Generate R per bundle; lockbox; wrap; split. Executor signs an engagement letter (condition definition, contact ladder, proof-of-life schedule, successor clause). Network policy created. Recipient kits hand-delivered (or staged if the user wants recipients unaware). A dummy **drill switch** (short timeout, garbage bundle) is armed through the identical legs. The arming record is sealed as a normal antseal unit.
- **Heartbeat:** `seal dms beat`, monthly; anyone may relay it. An optional duress key exists but the gate labels it honestly: weak against any coercer who has read this open design ("sign with both"); its real promise is that forced termination is slow and visible.
- **Reset:** is a beat. After silence begins: signature or recovery card only.
- **Terminate:** signed terminate posted; network policy deactivated; executor performs witnessed destruction of S_E with attestation; kits recalled best-effort; then rotate — new epoch, fresh R and shares — which, with shares-only custody, is real revocation for everything still recallable.
- **Release:** per-bundle timeout (personal tier 6–12 months; public 24) of verified silence, then the executor's contact ladder plus 60-day grace, then any two shares combine in the offline tool, outer unwrap, recipient's lockbox key opens the bundle, and the `.sealproof` verifies fully offline exactly as v1 specifies. The **annual live-fire drill** exercises this entire pipeline while the user can still repair a dead leg.

# 3. SCORECARD

1. **Early release:** two independent trust domains must collude, and for per-recipient bundles collusion buys only early delivery to the intended person. Beats all four. *Weaker spot:* public-release bundles are gated by executor+network alone — comparable to fixed LICHGATE, minus one network.
2. **Decades availability:** any single leg's death leaves a firing pair (E+R, N+R, E+N). Beats LICHGATE (either network's death kills it) and CENOTAPH-as-specified. *Loses to CAIRN* on "no young protocol anywhere" — one leg is a young network, priced as replaceable — and *loses to CENOTAPH's intent* on guaranteed unattended eventual fire, which MORTSAFE deliberately gives up.
3. **Condition integrity:** bimodal and quorum-corrected in both directions; beats every single-signal design. *Weaker spot:* human judgment (the executor) is inside the loop, by design.
4. **Coercion of infrastructure / legal:** one proceeding yields at most one share; executor and network occupy different legal categories and jurisdictions. *Weaker than LICHGATE* on one axis: MORTSAFE has a named human to subpoena at all.
5. **Coercion of the user:** forced beats work indefinitely — the ceiling of every revocable design; duress signaling is honestly labeled weak. No design beat this; those that claimed to were broken by their red teams.
6. **Privacy/metadata:** executor learns identity and existence; recipients learn at kit delivery unless staged; chain shows pseudonymous cadence with no public countdown events. *Worse than LICHGATE's* no-named-humans map; far better than CAIRN's total exposure.
7. **Complexity:** one Shamir, one AEAD, one lockbox scheme, calldata posting, one network policy, paper. *More than THREE ENVELOPES* — the network leg and kits are real cost; far less than two-network LICHGATE, and every piece tests against mocks.
8. **Recoverability/migration:** rotation is the native primitive and genuinely revokes (shares-only custody); the drill detects rot. *Weaker spot shared with all revocable designs:* it needs a living, attentive user; unattended-forever fire exists only via labeled irrevocable opt-ins (timelock, probate).

# 4. CHOSEN TRADE-OFFS

- **Recipient + one leg colluding** accelerates that recipient's own pre-consented bundle. Accepted: the harm is scheduling, not scope.
- **Executor + network colluding** publishes armed public-release bundles early. Accepted: optional class, flagged at consent, gated by two unlike domains.
- **Termination fails against two defectors** holding retained material. Accepted and printed at the gate; no design achieved cryptographic clawback without faking it.
- **A coerced user resets forever; incapacity past the timeout with a failed contact ladder fires while alive.** Accepted: inherent to revocable semantics; tiered timeouts, the recovery card, and the human ladder bound it.
- **No guaranteed fire** if the user dies, two legs are dead, and the drill lapsed. Accepted: delivery loss never touches the seal; the alternative (a public timelock share) was the round's worst break.
- **Murder/beneficiary incentive** is bounded by human verification and grace, not solved. No design solved it.
- **Executor metadata exposure.** The price of the decades leg.

# 5. WHAT ANALYSIS CANNOT SETTLE

1. **Can the network leg's condition be expressed at all?** TACo's declarative conditions must verify an Ed25519 signature over calldata with sequence monotonicity across a multi-RPC quorum view. Build it on TACo testnet; if declarative can't, measure a Lit Action; if neither, the leg falls to a second institution — a probe that can overturn an arm, this project's known pattern.
2. **Does TACo policy deactivation actually stop later share release against hoarding nodes** — measured behavior, not documentation.
3. **Recipient-kit cold usability:** hand kits to non-technical subjects and measure unaided claim success. Failure redesigns S_R.
4. **Will a real escrow agent and law firm sign letters containing signature-verification and proof-of-life duties, at what prefunded multi-decade price?** The medium is a quote from counsel, not an argument.
5. **Kit generation leakage:** instrumented build proving no share or lockbox key touches spool, temp, or printer flash; plant a fault to prove the check can fail.
6. **Network mortality probe:** TACo's own announced relaunch churn and drand health, measured before betting a leg.
7. **One full drill end-to-end** — testnet cohort, real envelopes, timed.

# 6. STAGED PATH

- **v1.1 (minimum worth shipping):** per-recipient bundles ONLY — the class where math caps every failure at "intended recipient, early." Human 2-of-3 (executor + recipient + user-chosen third party in another jurisdiction); no network leg, so nothing waits on probe 1. `seal dms {arm,beat,status,terminate,drill}`, lockbox kits, shares-only envelopes, calldata+OTS beats, recovery card, bimodal proof-of-life letters, the consent-gate wording above. The CLI refuses public-release arming, naming the roadmap.
- **v1.2:** network leg replaces the third human once probes 1–2 pass; rotation makes migration a re-arm, not a redesign.
- **v1.3:** public-release class (executor+network gate, executor-held ciphertext), watchtower daemon (any script that watches the chain and nags), funding-hygiene tooling, and the fixed-date timelock backstop as an opt-in stamped IRREVOCABLE at consent.
- **Throughout:** golden vectors and a tamper matrix for kit, share, and manifest formats; a passing drill required before any real switch arms.

## Vet findings (2026-08-15 — same-day check against MVP-SPEC.md; the judge's text above is verbatim and uncorrected)

Method: every claim below was checked against the frozen spec (MVP-SPEC.md, line numbers cited), not against the judge's own summary. Two overclaims, two ambiguities flagged, rest clean.

1. **Additivity above frozen format-v1 — PASS, with one terminology correction.** The layering wraps a *finished* `.sealproof` from the outside (lockbox, then outer AEAD, then Shamir), and release ends with "the `.sealproof` verifies fully offline exactly as v1 specifies" — no manifest, bundle, or CBOR schema is touched, and the trust map preserves the session's hard rules (W never leaves the vault; consent itemized at arming; worst case is the armed subset). But "the arming record is an ordinary sealed **unit**" is wrong as v1 vocabulary: v1 has no free-standing units — a unit exists only as part of a sealed file's decomposition, the unit `kind` set is closed (`normal | raw-mirror`, line 98), decoders hard-reject unknown fields (line 73), and an anchored manifest is immutable, so nothing can ever be added to an existing work. The additive reading, and the only possible one: the arming record is sealed as an ordinary **file in a new work** (its own W, manifest, payment, anchors). Same correction for §6's "tamper matrix for kit, share, and manifest formats": "manifest" there must mean the DMS arming-record format — antseal's manifest cannot change.
2. **Fresh HKDF label for the heartbeat key — PASS.** Line 77: info = `u8(len(label)) ‖ label ‖ LE64(id)` makes the (label, id) → info map injective *by construction*, "so any future label can be added safely" — this door was built for exactly this. Obligation attached: the new label must join the registered set covered by the M0 golden test that all HKDF infos are pairwise distinct (line 153). Scope note: W is per-work (line 89), so a "vault-derived" heartbeat key is per-switch/per-work — coherent with the per-switch-id statements, but any cross-work check-in identity would need new vault-level material (the wallet-key-under-its-own-sub-key precedent, line 143), which the verdict does not claim.
3. **"Hybrid X25519 + ML-KEM-768 … matching the project's hybrid-signature discipline" — OVERCLAIM.** The frozen hybrid discipline is *signatures only*: Ed25519 + ML-DSA-65 under `sig_policy` with context binding and strict verification (line 97). Format-v1 contains no KEM and no asymmetric encryption anywhere — all confidentiality is symmetric AEAD under W-derived keys, and bundles today reach recipients in plaintext. The lockbox is *philosophically consistent* with that discipline (classical+PQ pairing; and harvest-now-decrypt-later bites harder on a decades-horizon lockbox than it ever did on signatures), but it is genuinely new cryptographic surface: new crates and exact pins under the P7 pin-governance policy, RUSTSEC watch, a specified combiner for the two shared secrets (nothing in the spec defines one), golden vectors and tamper rows of its own, and recipient-side opening tooling that does not exist. "Consistent with the project's crypto philosophy" is earned; "matching the existing discipline" is not — no existing convention covers a KEM, and adopting one is its own decision round.
4. **"Public-release ciphertext stays OFF permanent public storage so terminate can claw it back" — PASS on consistency, one internal tension flagged.** Autonomi upload is "permanent, public, and irreversible" (line 34), so staying off it is *necessary* for any claw-back; this matches the spec and everything recorded in this session. Tension: §1 states the custody-hygiene rule absolutely ("institutions hold key shares only, never ciphertext") while §2.3 parks public-release ciphertext on executor-held media — the rule is really scoped to the per-recipient class, and the public class breaches it by necessity, priced at §3.1/§4. And §2.3's "genuinely claw it back" is bounded by the verdict's own §4 concession ("termination fails against two defectors"; the consent gate prints "best-effort"): off-permanent-storage makes claw-back *possible*, never guaranteed.
5. **Other checks, clean unless noted.** (i) *Per-recipient mirror to Autonomi* (§2.3): the exclusivity claim verifies — reading needs the outer R (any 2 of 3 shares) AND the lockbox private key, so no coalition excluding the recipient can ever read it — but "readable by the recipient alone" is exclusivity, not availability (the recipient alone holds one share and cannot rebuild R), and a mirrored copy permanently forfeits recall for that copy and ties an upload to a paying wallet (linkability risk, line 185) — it deserves the same consent-gate sentence the public class gets. (ii) *Chain facts*: correct — the EVM has no Ed25519 precompile (RIP-7212 covers P-256, not Ed25519) and ML-DSA verification on-chain is gas-prohibitive; signature-off-chain / chain-as-venue is the right call. (iii) *Recovery cards outside the vault*: they are pre-signed statements — signatures, not secret material — so no secret-custody rule is broken. (iv) *Positioning*: no "notary"/authorship/exclusivity language anywhere; "delivery loss never touches the seal" matches the spec's rule that evidence validity never depends on Autonomi (line 180). (v) *Staging nomenclature*: the verdict's internal v1.1/v1.2/v1.3 is the feature's own staging, not the tracker's tiers — in TODO.md the feature remains Later-tier, and the tracker's committed-v1.1 list is unchanged. (vi) *Probe count*: §5 names exactly seven, and probe 1 is written as able to overturn the network leg — the project's measured-medium pattern, correctly applied. (vii) *Trust-map arithmetic*: every listed coalition's capability was re-derived from the share/key layout and matches (including "executor + network → public-release bundles only", which follows from the public class having no lockbox).

Net: the architecture survives the spec check — strictly additive above format-v1 once "unit" is read as "file in a new work" — with §2.1's discipline claim and §2.3's claw-back phrasing corrected above, and nothing here re-opens the frozen format.
